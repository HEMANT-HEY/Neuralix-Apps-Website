Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Neuralix Apps Solutions handles information when you visit our website or contact us about a project.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Neuralix Apps Solutions (“we”, “our”, or “us”), based in Gurgaon, India, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the information we may collect, why we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

In brief: If you submit our project enquiry form, we use the details you provide to understand your requirements and respond to your enquiry. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number, company name, and preferred contact method, if you provide them.
  • Project information: project type, budget range, expected timeline, requested services, and details you include in your enquiry.
  • Messages and attachments: information you choose to send to us through a form or email.
  • Basic technical information: browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited, and diagnostic data, if website analytics or server logs are enabled.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through the project enquiry form.

2. How We Use Information

We may use submitted information to:

  • Review and respond to project enquiries.
  • Discuss project scope, estimates, timelines, and relevant services.
  • Communicate with you about a potential or ongoing project.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and services.
  • Maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations.

We will not use your enquiry details for unrelated promotional emails unless you have agreed to receive them or the law otherwise permits it.

3. How Enquiry Forms Are Handled

When you submit a project enquiry form, the information you provide may be transmitted to our business contact email, neuralixapps.official@gmail.com, so our team can review and respond.

Email is not guaranteed to be a secure method of transmission. Please share only information necessary for us to assess your project. The website owner should ensure the form is connected to a secure server-side form service or backend and that email credentials are never exposed in front-end code.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited information:

  • With team members or contractors who need it to assess or deliver your requested project and are expected to keep it confidential.
  • With service providers that support website hosting, form delivery, email, security, or analytics, where those services are used.
  • When required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Third-party services may process information under their own privacy terms. We encourage you to review those terms where relevant.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies to support functionality and security. If analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other optional tracking tools are added, they may collect usage information according to their own settings and policies. Where required, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies.

6. Data Retention

We keep enquiry and project information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, manage a business relationship, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the enquiry and any resulting project.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no website, email transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal information you provided.
  • Correct inaccurate contact or enquiry information.
  • Delete information that we no longer need to retain.
  • Stop sending non-essential communications.

To make a request, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your request before acting on it, and some information may need to be retained where legally required.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and project services are intended for businesses and individuals capable of entering into professional service discussions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or social media pages. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies separately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Please review this page periodically.
